Frequently Answered

Common Inquiries & Answers

Clear, direct answers for students and parents.

How does the 3-month (90 days) validity work?

Your 90-day timer begins immediately upon successful payment via Razorpay. During these 90 days, you can log in to your Student Portal anytime, anywhere, on any device to stream lectures as often as you want.

Can I download or screen-record the lectures?

No. All lectures are protected under studio-grade DRM. Our player embeds dynamic forensic moving watermarks stamped with your verified email, session hash, and live clock. Screen-recording software and downloading are strictly blocked.

What payment methods does Razorpay accept?

Razorpay supports UPI (Google Pay, PhonePe, Paytm), Debit Cards, Credit Cards, Net Banking across all Indian banks, and international payment methods.

Can beginners join these masterclasses?

Yes. Every masterclass begins with fundamental ergonomics (posture, hand mechanics, line grading) before advancing progressively to intermediate and performance-grade pieces.
