The Vision Behind Resynk Academy

Founded in 2014 on classical music theorem, psychological mindfulness, and conservatory discipline. Guiding aspiring musicians and artists into profound creative mastery.

Executive Direction

Academy Leadership

Guided by Master Rey and Sneha Sharma — uniting rigorous artistic theory with compassionate mentorship and conservatory excellence.

MR

Master Rey

Founder • Music Artist & Composer

With over a decade of pedagogical leadership, Master Rey has shaped the musical and creative trajectory of thousands of students across Karnataka. As an accomplished music artist and composer, he combines deep classical music theorem with empathetic psychological frameworks to transform mechanical rote learning into intuitive, expressive musicianship.

Certified Comprehensive Music Theorem — Classical harmony, modal counterpoint & score architecture
BA in Sociology — Societal artistic traditions, community cultural rituals & collective resonance
PGDP in Psychology — Cognitive retention, anxiety-free performance & accelerated musical learning
Certified Happiness Coach — Fostering emotional wellness, joy, and resilience through musical expression
SS

Sneha Sharma

Co-Founder • Operations & Strategy

As Co-Founder of Resynk Academy, Sneha Sharma directs academy operations, student experience, and our extensive institutional collaborations. Her strategic vision ensures that our curriculum stays modern, student progression remains structured, and every aspiring performer receives the personalized attention required to excel in guitar, keyboard, and fine arts.

Institutional Collaborations — Directing educational outreach across 15+ premier schools in Bangalore
Curriculum & Masterclass Operations — Orchestrating 90-day structured syllabi and student milestone tracking
Community Chapter Programs — Establishing dedicated arts chapters across luxury gated communities
Student Welfare & Mentorship — Maintaining an encouraging, inclusive conservatory culture
